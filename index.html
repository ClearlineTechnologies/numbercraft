THE LONG GAME

A game built around remembering.

Every skill has an explanation, a rule, and worked examples. Every game generates new rounds from valid mathematical rules.

01 · Understand
Read the lesson or open a worked example whenever you need it.

02 · Make the answer
The Answer Engine accepts numbers, decimals and fractions. It never evaluates an expression for you.

03 · Return to it
Missed questions come back. Successful recalls are scheduled further apart: a minute, ten minutes, a day, three days, a week, then three weeks.

04 · Keep growing
Earn XP, restore the observatory, and collect badges. Accuracy comes before speed. A timed mode is available when you want it.

Small multiplication and shape fact sets are finite and intentionally repeat. Across all tracks, randomized parameters create thousands of distinct problems and unlimited rounds. This game teaches and exercises its listed topics; it does not award academic credit or substitute for a full university degree.
